Principles

The landscape keeps changing. What we believe in doesn't.

P / 01
We do what's right for your company. That's our one true north - and we're passionate about it.
Every call, introduction and offer is weighed against one thing: is this the right move for the company? If the role shouldn't exist yet, we'll say so. If the candidate isn't right, we'll pull them.
P / 02
Your first hires will make or break your company. We treat them that way.
The people you hire in your first ten seats write the code, set the culture, and define what great looks like for everyone after. We hire founding teams.
P / 03
Senior people, on senior work. No junior sourcers learning on your role.
Every person you'll work with has held an in-house role at a company you've heard of, with hundreds of hires behind them. We don't farm out execution.
P / 04
Embedded means embedded. We use your tools, your brand, your standards.
Candidates speak to your recruiter. Your ATS is the source of truth. Your culture sets the bar.
P / 05
No hand-holding required. No back-and-forth on briefs.
We learn your stack, your culture, and the person who will thrive - then we take ownership of finding and closing them.
P / 06
We hire for where you're going, not where you've been.
We don't just match a CV to today's role. We find the people who'll still be the right hire as your company and its tools evolve.
P / 07
Hiring is a partnership on every front - you, us, and the candidate.
We work in lockstep with you on what the role needs, and we're honest with every candidate about the company, the trade-offs and the work.

Great hiring is a partnership. Let's start one.

Tell us about the team you're building. We'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit - and if not, who is.
