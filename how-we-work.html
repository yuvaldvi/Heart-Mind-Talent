Model 01

Agency.

Contingent · paid on success

The reality

Most agency recruiters haven't come from the best tech companies. The result is volume over quality - irrelevant profiles, wasted time, and a weaker brand with candidates.

When candidates have offers

When a candidate has multiple offers, an agency recruiter has limited influence on what they actually pick.

The fee dynamic

A success fee feels low-risk. Measured properly - in cost per hire and time to hire - it is usually the most expensive way to hire.

Model 02

In-house.

A single internal recruiter

The reality

One person juggling reqs, sourcing, scheduling, debriefs, offers and tooling. Time to hire stretches; cost per hire balloons.

Where it works

At a certain scale, an internal talent lead is valuable - mostly as connective tissue around an embedded team, not as the engine doing the work.

The hidden cost

Tooling, licences, ATS and employer-brand work all land on one cost centre before a single hire does. The economics rarely work below a certain headcount.

Model 03 · Ours

HMT.
Embedded.

Senior recruiters, inside your company

Hit the ground running

Our senior recruiters know the work, the industry, the candidates and the hiring managers. They bring AI agents and their own tooling. Day one is productive.

Credibility & closing power

They can sell the company, protect its brand, train hiring managers, and advise leadership on talent.

Economics that compound

As the company scales, time to hire and cost per hire both drop. With some of our customers, the cost per hire is less than 20% of the other models.

A typical engagement

From handshake to hire, in the shortest time the market will allow.

We meet founders and the hiring panel to align on the role, the bar, and the trade-offs. We pressure-test the job description, set scorecards, and benchmark compensation.

Step / 02
Embedding into your stack
Your dedicated recruiter works inside your tools - ATS, calendar, Slack, email on your domain. To candidates, they are your Head of Talent.
Week 1.

Sourcing & market mapping
AI agents source and screen at scale. We also tap our networks and surface passive candidates competitors can't reach. You see the shortlist, with notes, within days.
Weeks 1-3.
Step / 04
Interviewing & closing
We run the loop end-to-end: screens, scheduling, scorecards, debriefs, offers, references. You spend your time meeting strong people, not chasing calendars.

Top engineers trust recruiters who've hired and worked alongside people like them. That trust is what turns a shortlist into a signed offer.

Weeks 2 onwards.
Step / 05
Onboarding & review
We stay in the loop for the first 90 days, measuring quality-of-hire and adjusting the process. Most clients renew for the next wave of hiring.
Months 1-3 post-start.
